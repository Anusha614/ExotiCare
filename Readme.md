# ExoticCare Hub

ExoticCare Hub is a full-stack platform designed to help exotic pet owners **organize, manage, and understand the care of their pets**.

The project is being developed as a **production-style MERN application**, with the goal of gradually incorporating authentication, security, background jobs, caching, testing, CI/CD, and scalable backend architecture.

The long-term vision is to build an all-in-one platform for exotic pet owners, combining pet management, healthcare, AI-assisted guidance, useful care information, and a community in one place.

### What it will offer

- **Pet Management** — Manage pets, health records, feeding, reminders, and other important information.
- **Veterinary Care** — Find exotic-pet veterinarians and clinics, with the ability to request or book appointments.
- **Health & Diseases** — Explore species-specific diseases, symptoms, and health information.
- **AI Health Assistant** — An LLM-powered assistant that can use the platform's species, symptom, disease, and care data to help users understand their pet's symptoms, answer care-related questions, identify potentially relevant conditions, and recommend when professional veterinary attention may be appropriate. It will provide guidance, not replace a veterinarian.
- **Care & Nutrition** — Access species-specific information about food, habitat, and general care.
- **Community** — A social platform where exotic pet owners can post, ask questions, share experiences, follow others, and participate in communities.
- **Reminders & Notifications** — Keep track of feeding, medication, vet appointments, health checks, and other care tasks.
- **Discovery** — Discover species, veterinary services, communities, and other resources related to exotic pet care.

The goal is to create a platform where an exotic pet owner can **manage their pet, learn about its care, get AI-assisted guidance, find professional help, and connect with other owners — all in one place.**
